Overview

The project starts from candidate vote data, cleans it into CSV, calculates party totals, creates D'Hondt comparative figures, selects the top 20 candidates, and presents the result through an interactive HTML explorer.

Engineering problem

A simple personal-vote ranking is not enough for seat allocation. The result depends on party/list total votes, the candidate's rank inside that party/list, and the D'Hondt comparative figure.

Calculation method

From candidate votes to selected seats.

1. Candidate votesStart from cleaned candidate vote data.
2. Party totalsSum votes by party/list.
3. Party rankRank candidates inside each party.
4. Comparative figureDivide party total by internal rank.
5. Top 20Sort and select the highest figures.

Formula

comparative figure = party total votes / candidate rank inside party
